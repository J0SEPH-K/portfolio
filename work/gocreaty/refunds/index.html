Refund & Cancellation Policy

How GoCreaty plans are billed, how to cancel, what happens after you cancel, and when refunds apply. Last updated June 24, 2026.

Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how GoCreaty ("GoCreaty", "we", "us", or "our") bills its paid subscriptions, how you can cancel, what happens to your access after you cancel, and when refunds are available. It forms part of, and should be read together with, our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms of Service.

GoCreaty is offered in the United States. This Policy applies to all GoCreaty users. Where the law of your U.S. state of residence grants you mandatory rights that this Policy does not match, those mandatory rights prevail and this Policy is read to give them effect.

Plain-language summary: GoCreaty paid plans are monthly subscriptions that automatically renew every month until you cancel. You can cancel anytime in Settings, entirely online, with no phone call or email required. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the month you already paid for, and we will not charge you again. Fees for periods that have already begun are generally non-refundable, except where the law requires otherwise — for example, a pro-rated refund if you reject a price increase, as described below.

Plans and Pricing

GoCreaty offers one free plan and two paid subscription plans. All prices are in U.S. dollars (USD) and are charged per month. Prices exclude US state and local sales tax, which Stripe Tax may add at checkout based on your billing address; any applicable tax is shown on the checkout screen before you confirm your purchase and itemized on your receipt.

  • Free — $0/month. Up to 3 distinct video analyses per day. Sources: YouTube Shorts and TikTok. No charge and no payment method required. You can use the Free plan for as long as you like; it is a standing free tier, not a time-limited trial.
  • Pro — $9.99/month. Up to 50 distinct video analyses per day. Sources: YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Auto-renews monthly until cancelled.
  • Max — $29.99/month. Unlimited video analyses, subject to a fair-use soft cap of 300 analyses per day to prevent automated abuse. Sources: YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Auto-renews monthly until cancelled.

The Free plan never results in a charge and does not require a payment method. The remainder of this Policy concerning billing, renewal, and refunds applies to the paid Pro and Max plans.

GoCreaty analysis is AI-generated opinion and estimation — it is not a guarantee, prediction, or statement of fact about views, virality, or any other outcome. The number of analyses, plan features, and prices are the subject of this Policy; the quality or results of any analysis are not a basis for a refund except where required by law.

Automatic Renewal and Billing

Automatic renewal — please read. Your GoCreaty Pro or Max subscription is a recurring, automatically-renewing monthly plan. Before you provide payment information, we disclose to you, clearly and conspicuously at checkout: that your subscription will automatically renew and you will be charged on a recurring basis until you cancel; the price and monthly billing frequency; the date by which you must cancel to avoid the next charge; and how to cancel. By subscribing, you give your express, affirmative consent to these recurring-charge terms and authorize GoCreaty, through our payment processor Stripe, to charge your payment method the applicable plan price ($9.99/month for Pro or $29.99/month for Max), plus any applicable taxes, every month until you cancel. Charges recur on or around the same calendar day each month. There are no cancellation fees. You may cancel at any time from your Settings page, effective at the end of the then-current billing period.

Billed monthly, in advance

Paid plans are billed monthly in advance. The first charge occurs when you subscribe, and each subsequent monthly charge is made automatically at the start of each new billing period until the subscription is cancelled. Your subscription continues, and renews, until you cancel — it does not expire on its own.

Confirmation you can keep

After you subscribe, we provide an order summary and Stripe issues a payment receipt to your email. We recommend keeping these for your records. They restate your plan, the recurring price and monthly billing interval, that the plan auto-renews monthly until cancelled, your renewal date, and how to cancel. You can review these terms at any time in your account under Settings.

Renewal reminders

Where required by the law of your U.S. state — and as a courtesy to all paid users — we will send you a renewal reminder before a renewal where applicable. The reminder is sent to the contact method associated with your account and includes a description of your plan, the amount and frequency of the upcoming charge, the renewal date, and clear instructions for how to cancel before you are charged.

Price changes

If we change the recurring price of your subscription or other material terms, we will notify you clearly and conspicuously, using the contact method associated with your account, at least 7 days (and no more than 30 days) before the change takes effect, and we will tell you how to cancel. This includes any case where a referral discount ends and your price returns to the standard rate. If you do not cancel before the change date, the new price applies to your next renewal.

Your rights when a price goes up. Where the law of your U.S. state requires it (for example, New York and Colorado), we will either obtain your affirmative consent to the new price before charging it, or, if you do not agree, allow you to cancel and receive a pro-rated refund for the unused remainder of your then-current term if you cancel within 14 days of the first charge at the new price. In states that require advance notice of a fee change (for example, California), we provide that notice within the 7-to-30-day window described above.

How to Cancel

You can cancel your GoCreaty subscription at any time, entirely online, using the same method you used to sign up, with no need to call us, email us, or speak with anyone. Cancellation is at least as easy as signing up, in every U.S. state.

  • Go to your Settings page while signed in.
  • Open the billing/subscription controls and select Cancel Subscription.
  • Your cancellation is recorded immediately and takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

When you cancel, your subscription is set to not renew. We will not charge you again after the current paid period ends. Cancelling again is a harmless no-op. We do not impose retention hurdles, mandatory phone calls, or save-offer mazes that block you from cancelling; if we ever show you an optional offer to stay, a clear Cancel my subscription control is available on that same screen so you can complete your cancellation in one step.

What happens to your access: You keep full access to your paid plan until the end of the billing period you have already paid for. Access is not cut off at the moment you cancel — only future renewals stop. At the end of the period, your account moves to the Free plan unless you re-subscribe.

Refunds

GoCreaty subscriptions are billed in advance each month. You may cancel at any time; cancellation stops all future charges, and your access continues until the end of the current paid month.

No refund for elapsed or in-progress periods

Except where required by law, monthly subscription fees that have already been charged are non-refundable, and we do not provide partial or pro-rata refunds for unused time within a billing period. Because you retain access through the end of the period you paid for, cancellation does not trigger an automatic refund. Nothing in this Policy limits any non-waivable rights you may have under applicable U.S. consumer-protection law, including any mandatory cancellation or refund right your state grants — such as the pro-rata refund tied to a rejected price increase described in the Automatic Renewal and Billing section above. Where your state requires it, you may cancel and request a refund of the unused portion of your subscription, and we will honor that right. Taxes already collected and remitted may be non-refundable.

If we ever publish a specific refund or money-back guarantee on our pricing or checkout pages, we will honor it according to its stated terms. This no-refund posture is our stated policy and we apply it as written; it does not override any refund we are required by law to provide.

Goodwill and accidental or duplicate charges

As a courtesy, if you believe you were charged in error — for example, a duplicate charge, a charge after you cancelled, or a renewal you did not intend that you flag within 14 days of the charge — contact us at legal@gocreaty.com and we will review your request in good faith and respond within a reasonable time. Approved goodwill or error refunds are returned to your original payment method through Stripe. Reviewing a request does not waive the general no-refund posture above and does not create an entitlement to a refund except where the law requires one.

Nothing in this Policy affects your right to dispute a charge with your card issuer. The refund posture above is our policy with respect to GoCreaty; it does not limit any statutory chargeback or billing-dispute rights you have under federal or state law or your card network's rules. See the Payments, Chargebacks, and How Stripe Processes Payments section below.

Failed Payments and Account Status

If a scheduled renewal charge fails — for example, because your card expired, was declined, or has insufficient funds — we (through Stripe) may retry the charge over a short grace period and notify you so you can update your payment method in Settings. During the grace period you may continue to have access while we attempt to collect payment.

If payment cannot be collected after our retry attempts, your paid subscription will lapse and your account will move to the Free plan (up to 3 analyses per day). No cancellation fee or penalty applies for a failed payment. You can re-subscribe at any time from Settings, and your access to paid features resumes when a successful charge is processed.

Payments, Chargebacks, and How Stripe Processes Payments

How payments are processed

All payments are processed by Stripe, Inc., our third-party payment processor, via Stripe Checkout and the Stripe billing portal. Stripe holds your full payment card details and billing address; GoCreaty never stores your full card number. We retain only Stripe identifiers (such as your Stripe customer and subscription IDs) and, when needed to display your billing information, your card brand, last four digits, and expiry read live from Stripe. Your use of Stripe is also subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Chargebacks and payment disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first at legal@gocreaty.com so we can resolve it quickly — this is usually faster than a bank dispute. If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute with your card issuer, we may be asked by Stripe to provide records of your subscription, consent to auto-renewal, and usage. Filing a chargeback for a charge that was properly made under this Policy (for example, a renewal you did not cancel in time) may result in suspension of your account pending resolution. Initiating a dispute does not waive, and is separate from, any statutory rights you have, which you may exercise by contacting us directly.
