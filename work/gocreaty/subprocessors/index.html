About this page

GoCreaty surfaces trending public short-form videos from YouTube Shorts and TikTok and produces AI-generated analysis of why they are trending. To run this service we rely on a small number of carefully selected third-party providers. This page lists every third party that processes personal information on our behalf so you can see who is involved, what category of data each one receives, and where the processing happens. It supplements our Privacy Policy and is incorporated by reference into that policy.

We maintain this page as the single, authoritative source of truth for our vendor list. It was last updated on June 26, 2026 to describe the Creaty AI chat assistant, which sends your chat messages to Google's Gemini API. We may update it from time to time as we add, remove, or change providers; material changes will be posted here with a revised date, and where we add a new vendor that handles personal information we will note the change as described in the How we notify you of changes section below. If anything here conflicts with our Privacy Policy, the Privacy Policy governs the broader description of our practices and this page governs the specific provider list.

What a subprocessor is

A subprocessor is a third-party company that processes personal information on our behalf and under our instructions to help us deliver, secure, or operate GoCreaty. We act as the business that determines the purposes of processing, and each subprocessor acts as our service provider or contractor (as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act), bound by written contract to handle that data only for the limited and specified purposes we identify.

We distinguish two kinds of third parties on this page. First, subprocessors that receive or handle personal information about you, our account holder (for example, your sign-in identity or your payment details). Second, read-only public-data sources that we query to gather information about trending videos and other creators — these receive no GoCreaty account personal information, but we list them for transparency because the public data they return can itself contain other people's information (such as a creator's name, handle, or face in a thumbnail).

Important correction: no Cloudflare R2 and no managed object store

GoCreaty does not use Cloudflare R2 or any third-party object-storage service for your uploaded videos. When you upload your own short for analysis, the video file is stored on our own backend server's local disk, not handed to an outside storage vendor. We say this plainly because earlier descriptions of the product referenced R2; that is not how the current service works.

Subprocessors that handle your personal information

The following providers may process personal information about you as our subprocessors. For each we describe the purpose, the categories of data shared, and the processing region. Google Sign-In, the Gemini AI analysis, the Gemini-powered Creaty chat assistant, our hosting/database infrastructure, and Stripe are live, active providers in our production service. Optional fallback providers are inactive unless and until their credentials are configured — that caveat applies only to the fallbacks expressly labeled as such below, not to the core providers, which are always active.

Google LLC — Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect)

  • Purpose: User authentication. Google Sign-In is the only way to log in to GoCreaty.
  • Categories of data shared: During the OAuth sign-in handshake we receive your Google subject id, email address, display name, and profile picture (identity-only scopes: openid, email, profile). We do not request any YouTube, channel, or analytics scopes, and we never access or act on your own YouTube or TikTok accounts.
  • Region: United States (Google's U.S. infrastructure).
  • More information: Google Privacy Policy.

Google LLC — Gemini API (AI video analysis and the Creaty chat assistant)

  • Purpose: To generate the AI analysis — the model watches and hears the public video, or your own uploaded short, and returns the structured teardown — and to power the Creaty chat assistant, the Max-plan conversational feature that answers your questions about a video's analysis. This output is AI-generated opinion and estimation, not a fact, guarantee, or prediction.
  • Categories of data shared: For YouTube Shorts, the public watch URL is sent by reference (Google fetches the public video itself; our servers never download it). For your own uploaded video, the actual video bytes are sent for analysis; any copy held in Google's Files API is deleted immediately after the analysis call. For TikTok, only public metadata and signals are sent by default — no video bytes — unless the optional TikTok-watch feature is enabled, in which case bytes are fetched transiently and never stored. For the Creaty chat assistant, we send Google the messages you type, a rolling summary of the conversation, the relevant video's analysis brief, and — if you enable Creaty memory — the facts you chose to save; because chat is free text you control, please do not type sensitive personal information into Creaty. We do not send your account name or email to Gemini.
  • Region: United States (generativelanguage.googleapis.com).
  • Data-protection posture: Google processes this data as our service provider under Google's Cloud / Gemini API data-processing terms.
  • More information: Gemini API Terms.

Stripe, Inc. — payment processing and subscription billing

  • Purpose: Processing payments and managing subscriptions for our paid plans — Pro ($9.99/month) and Max ($29.99/month) — including Checkout, the billing portal, webhooks, and cancellation.
  • Categories of data shared: Your email address and an internal user id are sent to create a Stripe customer and Checkout session. Stripe holds the full payment card and billing address; GoCreaty stores back only Stripe customer/subscription/price ids and (read live, never stored in full) your card brand and last four digits. We never store your full card number.
  • Region: United States (Stripe's U.S. infrastructure).
  • More information: Stripe Privacy Policy.

Optional fallback providers (inactive unless configured). Depending on configuration, we may also engage an optional text-analysis fallback (Groq, Inc., United States) that receives only the analysis text prompt — public video signals and fenced creator metadata — and no GoCreaty account personal information. This fallback is inactive unless and until its credentials are configured, and it does not affect the four core providers above, which are always active. Where engaged, it is bound by the same confidentiality and CCPA-compliant data-protection terms described below.

Read-only public-data sources (no account data shared)

To build the trending feed and the analyses, we query the following read-only public sources. These receive no GoCreaty user personal information — requests are made with our own API keys or with public identifiers only, never tied to any user account or login. We list them for transparency because the public data they return is about other creators and can include third parties' personal information (names, handles, faces in thumbnails).

  • Google LLC — YouTube Data API v3 — read-only sourcing of public trending YouTube Shorts data (titles, statistics, thumbnails, creator info) using our server-side API key. No user data is sent. We honor the YouTube API's data-retention rule by auto-purging this third-party data after about 30 days. Region: United States. Terms.
  • TikTok metadata / MP4 resolver (tikwm.com) — read-only sourcing of public TikTok trending metadata and, when needed, transient resolution of a video URL. Only public TikTok identifiers are queried; no user data is sent. TikTok video bytes are fetched transiently and are never stored or re-served by GoCreaty. Region: operated by the third party; not controlled by GoCreaty.
  • Optional paid TikTok fallbacks (ScrapeCreators, TikHub) — used only if configured, as a fallback for public TikTok metadata when the free source fails. Only public TikTok queries; no user data is sent. These fallbacks are inactive unless their API keys are set.

TikTok videos are not stored. By default GoCreaty treats TikTok as metadata-only: no TikTok video file is downloaded, stored, or served. Where transient byte-fetching is enabled for analysis, the bytes are sent to the AI model and never persisted or re-served, and any temporary copy in the model's Files API is deleted immediately after analysis.

How we contract with our subprocessors

Every vendor that handles personal information for us is engaged under a written contract that binds it as a service provider or contractor under the CCPA. Those contracts include the protections described below; if a required term were absent, the law would treat the transfer as a sale or share rather than a service-provider disclosure, and we do not permit that.

Service-provider and contractor obligations

  • Purpose limitation. Each provider processes personal information only on our behalf for the limited and specified business purpose we identify for that provider (for example, cloud hosting of uploaded videos and account data, or AI analysis of a video), and not for any other purpose, outside our direct business relationship, or generically by reference to the overall services agreement.
  • No selling or sharing. Each provider is prohibited from selling or sharing the personal information.
  • No improper combination. Each provider is prohibited from combining the personal information with personal information it receives from, or on behalf of, any other person, except as the CCPA permits.
  • Same level of protection. Each provider must comply with all applicable CCPA obligations and provide the same level of privacy protection the CCPA requires of us.
  • Audit and oversight. We may take reasonable and appropriate steps to ensure each provider's use of personal information is consistent with our CCPA obligations, including assessments, audits, manual reviews, and automated scans, at least once every twelve (12) months.
  • Notice and remediation. Each provider must notify us promptly after determining it can no longer meet its CCPA obligations, and we may, on notice, take reasonable steps to stop and remediate any unauthorized use of personal information.
  • Contractor certification. Where a provider is engaged as a contractor, it certifies that it understands and will comply with these restrictions and its obligations under the CCPA, and that it will not retain, use, or disclose the personal information outside our direct business relationship or for any commercial purpose other than the specified business purpose, except as the CCPA permits.

Subcontractor flow-down

If a provider engages its own subcontractors to help process personal information for us, it must enter a written contract with each subcontractor that imposes the same obligations and restrictions set out above and otherwise complies with the CCPA and its regulations, so that those protections flow down the chain. The provider remains fully responsible to us for each subcontractor's performance.

Security

Each provider must implement and maintain reasonable security procedures and practices appropriate to the nature of the personal information, designed to protect it from unauthorized or unlawful access, destruction, use, modification, or disclosure, consistent with California Civil Code § 1798.81.5. On our request, a provider must provide documentation reasonably sufficient to verify its compliance, including current third-party assessments or audit reports, and must notify us without undue delay after discovering any security incident affecting the personal information. We do not treat these contractual commitments as a substitute for oversight; we exercise our audit and verification rights as part of ongoing vendor management.

Our commitments to you

Confidentiality and data-protection terms

We require every subprocessor that handles personal information to be bound by confidentiality and data-protection terms — the service-provider and contractor obligations described above — that obligate it to process the data only on our documented instructions, to protect it with appropriate security measures, and to assist us in meeting our obligations to you. We have data-processing terms in place with each subprocessor, and we engage subprocessors only where they meet these standards.

We do not sell or share your personal information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, in each case as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding 12 months. We do not load any third-party advertising, cross-site tracking, or behavioral-advertising SDKs, cookies, or pixels. We provide your personal information only to service providers and contractors who process it solely on our behalf for the limited purposes we specify, under written contracts that prohibit them from selling, sharing, retaining, using, or disclosing it for any other purpose. The providers listed here process your data only to operate the service, never to advertise to you or to sell your data.
